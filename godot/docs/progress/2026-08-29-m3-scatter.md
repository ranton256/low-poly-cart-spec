# M3 — the seeded field

**Status: the field exists, is reproducible from a seed, and every prop stands on
the ground.** Six GDD scenarios verified; M3's deferred count falls from 13 to 7,
the rest being collision and the camera shake.

## What the captures show

- `2026-08-29-m3-field.png` — seed **20260829** from the chase camera at the start
  line. The start area is clear, and the cottage on the left is a distant landmark
  without being asked to be: its clearance radius is 20 against everything else's
  8, which the design document says is why "cottages only ever appear as distant
  landmarks near the edges of the scattered field".
- `2026-08-29-m3-outer-ring.png` — driven past `scatterExtent`. Empty grass, no
  props, no grid lines, no boundary wall, which is the *Leaving an empty outer
  ring* scenario in one frame.

**On task 6.4 — do the extremes still read as the same asset?** Yes. The
`scaleVariation` range is 0.8–1.2 — a range nothing gated until this change's
review, and `check_tuning_transcription.py` now reads both ends from the World
table — so the widest spread any asset sees is ±20% of
its own size, and in the field capture a small tree and a large one are plainly
the same tree. The document asks for variation so the field does not look tiled,
not for silhouettes that change meaning, and ±20% is comfortably the former. The
narrow cottage range above is a sampling artefact of three instances, not a rule.

## Two bugs worth recording

**The generator sank every prop by half its authored height.**
`Normalise.with_variation()` operates on a box that is ALREADY grounded, so its
own `offset.y` is always zero — it is not the authored-space offset a view needs.
Using it directly put a tree 2.36 wu into the ground. The offset is now derived as
`box_final.position − authored.position × scale`, which reuses the specified path
rather than restating its arithmetic.

It was caught by the assertion the design predicted would matter: acceptance item
2 checked **at the extremes of the scale range and for all six scattered assets**, rather
than at whatever one scale a seed happened to produce.

**A capture came back with a pure-black sky.** The first outer-ring capture was
grabbed before the renderer had caught up: black sky, near-black ground, and two
re-runs of the identical command were correct. A capture tool that can silently
emit a half-drawn frame can silently produce misleading proof, so
`tools/drive_capture.gd` now waits 12 rendered frames instead of 3 and **refuses
to write** a frame more than 20% pure black.

## Reproducing

```sh
godot --headless -s tools/measure_scatter.gd          # the field as JSON
.venv/bin/python tools/check_scatter_conformance.py   # against the design document
godot -s tools/drive_capture.gd -- --out <path>.png --ticks 60  --hold ""
godot -s tools/drive_capture.gd -- --out <path>.png --ticks 420 --hold accelerate
```
