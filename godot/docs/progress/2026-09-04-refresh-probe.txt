and lap figures below are the 2026-09-01 record's to the last digit — so this
re-run exists for the COLUMN THAT IS NEW: the per-pass cue stream. The probe
grew two hashes per pass with this change, and acceptance item 16's first clause
("a scripted run's cue stream — ids, order, tick timestamps, and volumes — is
identical at 30, 60, and 144 frames per second") is asserted against this file by
tests/conformance_test.gd item 16, exactly the way item 14b is asserted against
the position and lap columns.

The two hashes answer different questions and both are reported per pass:

  tick hash    the probe's own FNV-1a fold over sim.cues.line(), sampled once per
               PHYSICS tick of the 3600-tick scripted drive. line() renders each
               record as id@tick:volume(x,z), so the fold covers exactly the four
               things the checklist names, in emission order.
  core digest  scripts/core/audio_cues.gd's own running digest over every record
               emitted since the pass began, the countdown included. It needs no
               sampling, so it cannot be defeated by one.

Neither hash is pinned as a literal anywhere. What item 16 asserts is that the
three RATES agree — the hashes are a function of the drive and the tuning table,
both of which move by proposal.
